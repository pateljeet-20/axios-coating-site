Two metals. One finish standard.

Whatever substrate you're bringing in, it leaves our floor with the same corrosion resistance, adhesion, and surface consistency.

Steel coating

Protective coating for structural steel, fabricated components, and formed parts — built to resist corrosion in outdoor and industrial-exposure conditions.

Aluminium coating

Finish systems suited to aluminium's lighter, more reactive surface — even coverage without compromising the base material's tolerances.

Bulk production runs

Our line is set up for volume — batch scheduling, palletised handling, and turnaround times that scale with the size of your order, not against it.

How a batch moves through the facility

Every order passes through the same four stages, whether it's fifty parts or five thousand.

  1. 01

    Surface preparation

    Incoming steel and aluminium is cleaned and prepped so the coating bonds properly — no shortcuts on this stage, since it decides how long the finish lasts.

  2. 02

    Coating application

    Parts are coated to spec across the full batch, with consistent thickness and coverage from the first piece to the last.

  3. 03

    Curing

    Coated components are cured under controlled conditions to lock in adhesion and durability before they're handled again.

  4. 04

    Quality check & dispatch

    Finished parts are checked for coverage and consistency, then staged for pickup or dispatch to your site.
